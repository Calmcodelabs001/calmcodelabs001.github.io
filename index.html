Privacy Policy

CalmCode Labs
Effective Date: May 8, 2026

CalmCode Labs ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Image & Video Compressor mobile application (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect user information when you use our App.

1. Information Collection

Our App does not require account registration or login and works primarily offline on your device.

We do not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos from your device
  • Precise location data

However, the App may use Firebase services provided by Google LLC to improve app performance, stability, and user experience.

Firebase may automatically collect limited non-personal information such as:

  • Device type
  • Android version
  • App version
  • Crash reports
  • Anonymous usage analytics

This information is used only to improve app functionality, fix technical issues, and enhance performance.

2. Storage & Media Permissions

The App requests storage and media access permissions only to allow users to:

  • Select images and videos for compression
  • Save compressed files to the device
  • Access previously compressed media
  • Manage compression history inside the App

Your media files are processed locally on your device. We do not upload, collect, store, or share your personal photos or videos on external servers.

The App only accesses files that you explicitly choose within the application.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent users' personal information.

Anonymous technical information collected through Firebase services may be processed by Google according to their own privacy policies.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob (if ads are enabled)

These services may collect anonymous technical information necessary for app functionality, analytics, crash reporting, and advertising support.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security

We value user privacy and take reasonable measures to protect app-related data and ensure secure operation of the application.

All media compression operations are performed locally on the user's device whenever possible.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
