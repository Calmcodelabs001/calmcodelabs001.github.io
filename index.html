Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Are CalmCode Labs apps free?

Yes, our apps are free to download. Some apps show ads to support development, and some may offer optional premium features.

Where can I download your apps?

All our apps are available on the Google Play Store under the developer name CalmCode Labs.

Do your apps need an internet connection?

Many features work offline. Some features, such as online translation, need an internet connection.

Why does an app ask for permissions?

Permissions are only used for the feature you are using — for example, the camera for flashlight or image translation, or storage for saving files.

How can I report a problem or suggest a feature?

Email us at the address below or leave a review on Google Play. We read every message.
